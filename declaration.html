1. Introduction

Welcome to https://startyourstory.in ("Start Your Story"), a recruitment platform owned and operated by NEXTSTEP SOLUTIONS. We strive to provide a secure, fair, and efficient platform for CA Firms and Students to connect and avail digital services. This declaration outlines the rules and guidelines that all users must adhere to while using our services.

The platform deals exclusively in digital services and subscriptions, including Premium Membership Plans for CA Firms, Platform Wallet Recharge Services, the CA Library (digital learning resources), and Resume Builder & Career Services. We do not sell physical products, and payments are received only for digital services and subscriptions.

2. Definitions

  • Recruitment Platform ("Platform"): The online platform provided by NEXTSTEP SOLUTIONS through https://startyourstory.in under the brand name "Start Your Story" for offering and delivering digital services, including recruitment, digital learning, and career services.
  • User: Any individual or entity registered on the Platform, including both CA Firms and Students.
  • CA Firm: A user (Chartered Accountancy firm) that subscribes to Premium Membership Plans, uses Platform Wallet Recharge Services, and avails recruitment-related digital services through the Platform.
  • Student: A user who accesses the CA Library (digital learning resources), uses the Resume Builder & Career Services, and applies to opportunities published by CA Firms through the Platform.
  • Digital Services: The services offered on the Platform, namely Premium Membership Plans for CA Firms, Platform Wallet Recharge Services, the CA Library (digital learning resources), and Resume Builder & Career Services.
  • Transaction: Any agreement or exchange of digital services, subscriptions, or funds between users and the Platform, processed through the Platform. Payments are received only for digital services and subscriptions; no physical goods are bought, sold, or shipped.
